One short, funny rant about a small everyday annoyance — a smoke detector
that only ever complains at 3 a.m. — written for make.fun's
[Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046).
A furious judge reads rants two at a time and picks the one that lands
hardest; this one is capped at 400 characters, so the punch comes fast.

## The rant

> My smoke detector only ever beeps at 3 a.m. It has a full battery, a clear
> ceiling, and nine whole hours of daylight to raise its concerns. Instead
> it waits until I'm fast asleep, then chirps once — like a tiny landlord
> reminding me who really owns the night.

(259 characters.)

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt` (the program/v1 harness
artifact) and prints it to stdout.
